Ormma

Delete your Ormma account and data

Last updated: 2026-11-16.

This page explains how to delete your Ormma account and the data linked to it. Ormma is a family care app that phones an elderly parent at medicine times and shows the reply to the family.

Delete your account in the app

1. Open Ormma and sign in with your phone number. 2. Go to the Family tab and tap Settings. 3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you are the owner of a parent, delete each parent you own first (Family tab, the parent's card, Delete parent). The app asks you to do this before it deletes the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your phone number sign-in, your name and your profile.
  • Your place in every family circle you belong to, and the invite codes you created that were not used.
  • Your plan record: the Ormma Family plan, product and end date, and the hash of the purchase token.
  • When you delete a parent you own: the parent, their medicines, dose history, alerts, the family call log and the emergency contacts, and any prescription image still waiting for review.

What is kept

  • Google Play payments: a Google Play subscription is cancelled in Google Play (Play Store, Payments and subscriptions). Google keeps its own payment records. We never see card or bank details.
  • Backups and logs: short-lived copies in backups and server logs are removed on their normal schedule. The logs hold IDs and statuses only, never medicine names or health values.
  • Other members: deleting your account does not delete a family circle that another owner manages.

You cannot open the app

Write to rejiranair@gmail.com from, or naming, the phone number on the account, with the subject "Delete my Ormma account". We will ask you to confirm the number, then delete the account and its data and reply when it is done.

Request a copy of your data first

An owner can export a parent's data from the Family tab (Export data) before deleting.

For how data is handled, read the privacy policy.

Ormma records and relays. It does not give medical advice.
