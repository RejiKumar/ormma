Ormma privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05.

What Ormma is

Ormma phones an elderly parent at medicine times, records whether the dose was taken, and shows the result to family members in an app. Ormma records and relays. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or suggest a dose, a time or a medicine.

What we collect

  • Family members: phone number (for sign-in), display name, a push notification token, and the time you last opened the Today screen. Other members of the same parent can see your name and when you were last active. You may add your relationship to the parent and your city, and choose which notifications you receive (missed-dose alerts, the 8 PM summary, quiet hours). Other members also see when you have viewed a dose update ("Seen by"). We do not collect location.
  • The parent: name, phone number, language, time zone, consent status, the medicines and dose times the family enters (including how the pill looks and what it is for), the parent's city, the prescriber's name and the refill date, whether each dose was taken, and a short note about the reply.
  • Calls: we process the parent's spoken reply to understand it and do not keep the audio. Transcripts are kept only as needed to show the reply to the family.
  • Emergency contacts: a name, a type (neighbour or clinic) and a phone number that an owner chooses to add, with an optional short note, so family members can call them if a dose is missed.
  • Family call log: when a member taps "I called", we record who and when.
  • Subscription: if you subscribe, Google Play takes the payment. We keep the plan, the product, the end date and a one-way hash of the purchase token. We never see or keep card or bank details.

How we use it

To place reminder calls, show the family how the day is going, alert the family when a dose is missed, and keep the account secure. We do not sell data and do not show ads.

Who can see it

Only members of that parent's family circle. If consent is withdrawn, members other than the owner stop seeing information dated from that point on.

Service providers

Google (Firebase, Speech-to-Text, Text-to-Speech), Exotel (phone calls), Anthropic (to structure a reply into a status; calls are made from our servers and no phone number is sent) and Google Play (payments). Data is stored in Mumbai (asia-south1).

Your choices

An owner can export a parent's data and delete a parent and its data from the Family tab. Deleting removes the parent, medicines, dose history, alerts, family call log and emergency contacts. For anything else, write to the contact address below. In Settings you can change your name, switch between light and dark appearance, and delete your account, which removes your profile, your place in every family and your plan record. You must delete the parents you own first. A Google Play subscription is cancelled in Google Play.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by role (owner, editor, viewer) and checked on every request. Secrets are kept in Secret Manager, never in the app.

Children

Ormma is for adults. It is not directed at children.
