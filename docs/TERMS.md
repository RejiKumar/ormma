## What Ormma does

Ormma phones a parent at medicine times, records whether the dose was taken, and shows the result to the
family in an app. Ormma records and relays what the parent says. It is not a medical device.

## No medical advice

Ormma does not provide medical advice. It does not diagnose, suggest or change a dose, a time or a medicine,
and it does not judge a reading. Decisions about health stay with the parent and their doctor. If someone
may be in danger, call your local emergency number. Do not rely on Ormma as the only way to check on a person.

You sign in with your phone number. You are responsible for what happens under your account and for the

information shared with the family. Only add a parent if you have their consent.

## Families and roles

The person who adds a parent is the owner. Owners can invite members as editors or viewers and can remove
the parent and its data. Members can see what the owner's settings allow.
